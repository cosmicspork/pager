Script injection

Off means the scripts are never injected — the extension registers content scripts from these toggles rather than loading them and checking a flag. Already-open tabs keep the old scripts until you reload them.

What Teams pages you for

One-to-one and group chats.
Mentions are read from Teams' own index, so one still reaches you after other people have replied on top of it.
Busy during a call and rarely worth a buzz, so off by default.

Anything older than ten minutes is ignored regardless — Teams re-syncs a pile of conversations on startup and on reconnect, and without that floor every one of them would look new.

Keep Teams active

A synthetic mouse move and Shift keypress, this often. Teams goes idle at about five minutes, so keep this under 300.

Bridge

Must be http on localhost or 127.0.0.1 — the bridge holds the signing key and is not meant to be reachable off this machine.
